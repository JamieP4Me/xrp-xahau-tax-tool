Monthly Trading Price
The monthly high is the highest daily high in that month and the monthly low is the lowest daily low, from the same embedded CoinGecko data this tool uses to calculate cost basis and gains. Spread is high − low, in dollars and as a percentage of the low — how far the price travelled within the month, which is the range your cost basis could have fallen in depending on the time of day a transaction settled. Market volume is everything traded everywhere that month. Your volume is what moved into or out of your own wallets. Prices aggregate by extreme — a year's high is its highest month's high — while volumes aggregate by sum. No Payment-Claim, no network request.
Two of these columns are estimates, and it matters which. Market Vol ($) is reported data. Market Vol (est.) is not: the source gives dollars only, so units are derived by dividing by the month's midpoint price — in a month where the price moved sharply, that is out by tens of percent. Your Vol in units is exact, straight off the ledger; its dollar figure is estimated the same way, because the ledger records no price and the time of day is not recoverable from a daily series. A † marks a period the data does not fully cover — the current month always, and any year containing it.

None of this touches your tax figures. Volume is context. Cost basis and proceeds come from real FIFO lots and are unaffected by everything on this page.

Why this is not sourced from an XRPL explorer. XRP's volume does not happen on the XRP Ledger. The XRPL DEX order book runs on the order of 3.6M XRP a day against a market of roughly $2.5bn a day, so a ledger explorer — Bithomp, XRPScan, or anything else reading the chain — sees about 0.2% of it. Market volume has to come from an exchange aggregator, and does.
How to Set Up Dhali + WinDB for Future Years
1Get a Xaman wallet with XRP

You need a small amount of XRP to fund the Dhali payment channel. Just 2–5 XRP is plenty for years of queries. Download Xaman at xaman.app if you don't already have it.

2Open the Dhali Payment Terminal for WinDB

Go to: pay.dhali.io/?uuids=8d0eb161-551f-4471-bae7-ff9ef205d62b
This URL pre-fills the WinDB API UUID so your payment authorizes specifically WinDB queries.

3Fund the channel & generate a Payment-Claim

1. Connect your Xaman wallet in the terminal
2. Fund a payment channel with ~2–5 XRP (you can close it and reclaim unused XRP at any time)
3. Click "Generate Payment Claim" — you'll get a long base64 string starting with eyJ...
4. Copy the entire string

4Paste the Payment-Claim into Setup

Paste it into the "Payment Claim" field in Setup. Cost per query: ~0.000423 XRP (~$0.001). Fetching all of your wallets costs relatively little — the SQL covers each wallet's complete history in a small number of paginated queries.

5Future tax years — just change the year and re-fetch

For 2026 taxes: change the Tax Year dropdown to 2026, update your price data, and click Fetch. The WinDB database continuously indexes all new Xahau and XRPL transactions so future years work automatically. Top up your XRP payment channel when the balance runs low.
